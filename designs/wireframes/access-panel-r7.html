Access panel, round 7: the editor pointer

While a section is being edited, the editor says in one short line when the unsaved change would make an Access notice for a watch, and offers Open. The Access tab then shows that watch with a what-if line: the health line it would have if the change were applied. Apply turns the line into an ordinary notice; Cancel removes it. Double-click a card in Section View to edit it: change a meeting's day or start, the section type or the campus. Revision 7b (Greg's answers): stranded sections speak too, and dragging a meeting on the grid shows the same lines while the meeting is held over a spot.

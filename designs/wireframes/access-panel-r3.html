Access panel, round 3: some courses, pins and stepping

Enviro Y2 is open. Untick a course to see access for students who take fewer; right-click a section line on the grid to pin it; press Step through slates in the grid's legend bar to see one slate at a time (arrow keys step). Semester changes moves PSYCH 101 sections as before. Nothing you tick, pin or step is saved: closing the watch resets it, and it all works read-only.

Changed in 3b, from your round 3 review

  • The "any 3 of these" line is gone: ticking courses covers the specific cases users care about.
  • Right-click to pin stays.
  • The legend bar is cleaned up: no key to the marks (●, ◆, ▲, hatching). It holds only the watch's name, Step through slates, Show other courses and Close. Each mark on the grid explains itself on hover.
  • Section names in messages are clickable (▲ and ◆ lines, pin chips, the stepper's slate). They look like plain text until hovered (underline, hand cursor). A click selects the section as anywhere else: highlighted on the grid, and Section View scrolls to its card. Clicking a line on the grid selects too.
  • Yellow cautions (◆) from each section's share of slates, compared with its fair share in its group (1 ÷ sections in the group). Hard to reach: under a third of its fair share. Heavy load: over twice it, in a group of 3 or more. Sections with no times (online) never get one. Open Chem Y2: "CHEM 201 LAB-A fits only with LEC1: it clashes with LEC2, LEC3 and LEC4." Then pick the last Semester changes option for heavy loads. Like stranded sections, cautions don't change the watch's mark.
  • Stranded sections are flagged, not just hatched on the grid: "1 section stranded" in the line, and a ▲ line in the detail giving the reason (As planned: PSYCH 101 C clashes with CHEM 213 A). The watch's mark in the list doesn't change for them alone: a stranded section may be meant for other students.
  • The stepper moved to the grid's legend bar: Step through slates, then ‹ Slate 2 of 11 › with the slate's sections, and Clear to go back to the overview.

Round 3 as before

  • Tick boxes beside each course. Unticking re-runs everything (line, slates, seats, grid) on the ticked courses; the line starts "3 of 5 courses". At least one stays ticked.
  • Pins: right-click a section line on the grid, then Pin in slates. Only slates with that section count. Pins show as chips under the courses (✕ removes one). Pinning a section of an unticked course ticks it.
  • Stepping is in a stable order (course order, then section code). The slate's sections get the Room Browser's green ghost look, line by line; the watch's other sections fade. Changing ticks, pins or the semester goes back to slate 1.

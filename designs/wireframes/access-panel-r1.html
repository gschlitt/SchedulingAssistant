Access panel, round 1: the watch list

A low-fidelity mock of TermPoint's main window at real size. Mock data only. Round 1 judges the Access tab's watch list, the filter-bar summary, and popping the tab out. Clicking a watch only says what round 2 will do.

Round 1 settled (Greg, 2026-11-10)

  1. At 250 px you can tell at a glance which watches need you.
  2. One line per watch, wrapping to the tab's width (no cut-off text).
  3. Marks help. ▲ and ○ stay; ● stands in for a "fragile" icon (like the broken-glass packaging symbol), to be found later.
  4. The notices box at the top is the right weight.
  5. The filter-bar summary points to the tab well enough.
  6. Popping out the tab in front from the tab strip feels right.

Not in this round

  • A watch's detail and the grid picture (round 2)
  • Stepping through slates and pins (round 3)
  • Adding and editing a watch (round 4)
  • Courses, Copy Semester, Check all courses (later rounds)
  • Real fonts and controls: Avalonia's will differ a little

Access panel, round 6: conflict marks and delivery type

Two pieces. Conflict marks (the spec's "teal boxes"; in the app they are a red glow on each conflicting section's line, with a red line joining them across tiles) now mark only conflicts that cost students something, and say so on hover. Revision 6b: Mark all conflicts (Access tab, off by default) marks every conflict as today. Revision 6c: a watch's Courses list says which sections it uses (open the tag watches Cohort 1 and Cohort 2). Delivery type is a new optional section field: how a section is taught. Double-click a card in Section View to edit it; it also shows on cards, as a filter, in Scheduling Environment and in Copy Semester (both on the menu bar). Wording is proposed, for approval.

New in round 6

  • Revision 6e (Greg): a Multi-campus box per watch, off by default. It sits under the courses in a light font, and only on a watch whose in-person sections are on more than one campus (open Enviro Y2). Off, a slate keeps to one campus, as in 6d: a section on the other campus is stranded ("STAT 104 C is stranded: it is at Chilliwack, and every slate uses BIOL 201 A at Abbotsford (Multi-campus is off)"). Ticked, slates may mix campuses: a quiet count ("11 slates · 5 mix campuses"), "mixes campuses" on each such slate while stepping, and, when every slate mixes campuses, the yellow ● and the cause in yellow. Try Semester changes 6 (CHEM 213 A moved to Chilliwack) on Enviro Y2: off, "No full slate · CHEM 213 A is at Chilliwack, but BIOL 201 A is at Abbotsford" (hover says to tick Multi-campus); ticked, "11 slates · every slate mixes campuses · CHEM 213 A is at Chilliwack, but BIOL 201 A is at Abbotsford". The box is saved with the watch.
  • Revision 6d: why there's no full slate. Try Semester changes 5 to 7. (5) A course that can't be taken even alone: "BMEG 230's labs all conflict with its only lecture (101)", not "can't fit with the rest". (6) Campus, with Multi-campus off (see 6e). (7) More than one course left out: "CHEM 213 and GEOG 250 can't fit" when there's one way, "CHEM 101 or MATH 111 can't fit" when either, and here "only one of CHEM 213, GEOG 250 and BIOL 201 fits". Cohort 3 mocks a missing part: its BIOL 201 lab isn't tagged, so the line reads "No full slate · BIOL 201's labs aren't in this watch · the other sections fit together".
  • Revision 6c: a watch covers exactly the sections its filter shows, so a tag on chosen sections makes a cohort watch. Two are added as tag watches. Cohort 1 takes some sections of four courses; its Courses list gives one phrase per part: "all 3 sections", "only A", "all but L2", "LEC1, LEC2", or "4 of 8 ▸", which opens a line naming them and the ones left out. "All" counts the whole semester, not the grid's filters. Cohort 2 takes one section of each of three courses: its line reads "All 3 sections fit together", or, with PSYCH 101 A moved to Tue Thu 10:00, "CHEM 213 A conflicts with PSYCH 101 A", with no slate count, best partial or Step through slates.
  • Revision 6b (Greg's review): Mark all conflicts, in an open watch's bar above the schedule (and at the foot of the watch list, for when none is open), off by default. Ticked, every conflict between two parts of a watch is marked as today (in As planned, 30 instead of 12 since 6c–6d (was 22 instead of 2), including lecture–lab conflicts inside CHEM 201), since removing one may still improve the schedule, or matter in ways TermPoint can't know. The extra ones look the same; their hover says "Other sections still let students in 1st yr Science get around this conflict."
  • Conflict marks (rule 33): only conflicts that cost students something: a section no one taking the watch's courses can use, or no way to take every course. Hover a red line (or the red line between tiles) for what it costs. Try each Semester changes option. The last one is the Q22 case: no single conflict is to blame, so every conflict with CHEM 213 A is marked.
  • Marks show for every watch when none is open; with a watch open, only that watch's marks. Ticks and pins don't change them: they show the saved schedule.
  • What's new appears once after the upgrade, in the app's announcement banner (top of the window), so people used to every overlap glowing don't think marks have gone missing.
  • Delivery type: double-click a card in Section View. Delivery Type sits beside Campus (both say how and where a section is taught), optional. Cards show it after the section type ("Lecture · Blended"). The filter bar gets Delivery Type. Scheduling Environment gets a Delivery Types list after Section Type; Section Type and Meeting Type get descriptions that say how the three differ. Deleting one follows round 5's preset rule (try Online live with the preset Online sections). Copy Semester gets "Include any delivery type assignments", ticked.
  • Not shown: Import Sections gets an optional Delivery Type column (new names are added to the list, as Section Type does today), and exports include it.
